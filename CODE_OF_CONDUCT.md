# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful space for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful
- Welcome differing viewpoints and give constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without their permission

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email **jira@serso.pt**. All reports are reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or remove anyone whose behavior harms the community, depending on severity.
